Live slots

Slots that move with the aircraft.

OpsRamp follows each inbound aircraft with live flight tracking. When the live estimated ETA moves, the estimated departure, the Box Time slot and the truck departure move with it. Nobody has to call the kitchen.

  • Live estimated ETA and ETD, refreshed from live aircraft tracking (ADS-B)
  • Box Time and truck slots recalculated from your own timing rules
  • The change shows on every screen at once, with an alert if a slot gets tight

Live estimates are computed from the real position of the aircraft. They are not official airline schedules and can move as the flight progresses.

    Data & privacy

    Your data stays yours.

    OpsRamp is designed around five principles. They shape how the product is built, not just how it is sold.

    • 01

      An isolated workspace

      Each customer has its own workspace and its own database. Your data is never visible to another customer.

    • 02

      Never sold, never used to train AI

      Your schedule is used to run your board. It is never resold and never used to train AI models.

    • 03

      Encrypted in transit and at rest

      Data is encrypted on its way to your screens and where it is stored.

    • 04

      Access per user, with roles

      Admin, supervisor, and read only for TV screens. Everyone sees what their role needs.

    • 05

      Export and deletion on request

      Ask for a full export of your data, or for its deletion, at any time.

    • How each principle is applied: hosting, encryption, access, retention.

    FAQ

    Questions operators ask

    Is our schedule data secure?

    Each customer has an isolated workspace with its own database. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is per user with roles, and no passenger data is needed. The details are on the Security & privacy page.

    Where do the live ETA and ETD come from?

    From live aircraft tracking (ADS-B). OpsRamp estimates arrival and departure times from the real position of the aircraft. These are live estimates, not official airline schedules, and they can move as the flight progresses.

    Who pays for the AI?

    You do, through your own AI subscription. OpsRamp only bills access and setup, never AI usage.

    Do we need an IT project or special training?

    No. OpsRamp runs in a web browser on PCs, phones and TVs. We create your workspace with you, and your flights and timings go in the config panel. A short walkthrough is enough for the team.

    Can we adapt it to our operation?

    Yes. Airlines, colours, meal types, alert timing and the rules behind sealing and truck slots, per aircraft size, are all set in your workspace.

    What support do we get?

    Setup is done with you, not handed over as a manual. Email support is included.

    Can we try it first?

    Yes. Book a demo and we can set up a trial on your own schedule so your team sees real flights.
