FAQ

Questions operators ask

Is our schedule data secure?

Your data stays in your own Google Sheet, under your Google account permissions. OpsRamp only reads the published schedule. No passenger data is needed.

Where is it hosted?

OpsRamp is a web app served over HTTPS from a global CDN. Nothing to install on your servers. Private hosting can be discussed on the Enterprise plan.

Can we adapt it to our operation?

Yes. Airlines, colours, alert timing, meal types and views are configured for your site during setup.

What support do we get?

Email support on every plan, with priority response on Operations. Setup is done with you, not handed over as a manual.

Does it integrate with our systems?

It starts from a Google Sheet, which any system can export to. Direct connections to other data sources are available on Enterprise.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Book a demo and we can set up a trial on your own schedule so your team sees real flights.
